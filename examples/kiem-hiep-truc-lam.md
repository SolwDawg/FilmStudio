Bối cảnh: Rừng trúc dưới mưa
Shot: (Wide shot, slow push-in) Misty bamboo forest in steady rain. Lăng Phong stands still on a muddy path in a dark teal robe and bamboo hat, left hand on his black-sheathed sword. Leaves rustle behind him; he slowly turns his head and draws the sword in one calm, steady motion. The shot ends in a medium shot on his face under the hat, the drawn sword low in his right hand.

Nhân vật: Lăng Phong, Mộ Dung Tuyết
Bối cảnh: Rừng trúc dưới mưa
Shot: (Wide tracking shot) Misty bamboo forest in steady rain. Lăng Phong holds his drawn sword. Mộ Dung Tuyết, in layered white robes with a white face veil, runs in from the left across the mud, splashing through puddles, and lunges with both short swords; Lăng Phong turns and sweeps both blades aside, and they slide back into guard stances a sword's length apart.

Nhân vật: Lăng Phong, Mộ Dung Tuyết
Bối cảnh: Rừng trúc dưới mưa

Nhân vật: Lăng Phong, Mộ Dung Tuyết
Bối cảnh: Rừng trúc dưới mưa
Shot: (Wide shot, low angle) Misty bamboo forest in steady rain. Mộ Dung Tuyết steps in with a wide horizontal slash; Lăng Phong pushes off a bamboo trunk, arcs over her and lands behind her on the mud, knees bending to absorb the landing, his sword tip stopping beside her shoulder.

Nhân vật: Lăng Phong, Mộ Dung Tuyết

She slowly turns to face him and her wet veil slips off, revealing her face; Lăng Phong lowers his sword. Lăng Phong: "Tiểu sư muội?" Mộ Dung Tuyết: "Sư huynh còn nhớ lời thề năm xưa?"

SCENE 7:
Nhân vật: Lăng Phong, Mộ Dung Tuyết
